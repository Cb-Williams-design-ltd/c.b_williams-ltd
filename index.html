Which replacement parts are used?

Availability varies by model. Your quote will identify the proposed part, such as a compatible aftermarket or genuine service part where available, and explain any relevant differences. Genuine manufacturer parts are not assumed at these guide prices.

What should I send for a quote?

Include the make and exact model, what is wrong, whether it still powers on and a photo of visible damage if possible. For example: “iPhone 12, cracked screen, touch still works.” You do not need to send your passcode or account password.

04 / Ongoing costs

What do you pay for?

The build is a one-off project. Your web address and any paid hosting renew separately.

Domain name

Allow around £10–£30 / year

Your address, such as yourbusiness.co.uk. This is a planning allowance for an ordinary domain, not a registrar quote; premium names can cost much more. Check the renewal price and VAT, not just the first-year offer.

Website hosting

Free options · paid plans quoted separately

Hosting keeps your website files online. A simple brochure site may suit a free service such as Cloudflare Pages, subject to its limits. Content management, shops and other features may need a paid plan. We confirm the provider and recurring charge before you commit.

Changes after launch

Small edits: estimated £20–£40

For a small agreed batch of text or image changes. New pages, redesigns and added features are quoted separately. Ongoing maintenance is optional and is not included in the build or hosting fee.

Example budget

A £250 one-page build + a £20 annual domain + eligible free hosting = £270 in year one, then £20 per year at the same domain renewal price, before optional updates or extras. Illustrative only; provider prices can change.

Do I own my domain?

Register it in your own name or business name, using an account and email address you control. Registration gives you the right to use it for the registration period; renew it to keep it. You can change your website or hosting without changing the domain.

What about HTTPS and business email?

HTTPS encrypts the connection between visitors and your site. Many hosting services include the certificate; any extra charge will be confirmed in your quote. A mailbox such as hello@yourbusiness.co.uk is a separate service unless your provider includes it. Email links on a website do not create a mailbox.

What affects the final build price?

The number of pages, design detail, content preparation, revisions and extra features all affect the work involved. Tell us your budget and what the site needs to do so we can give you a clear scope and quote. Search setup does not guarantee Google rankings.
